How to Play

Guess the daily Pikmin enemy!

Each guess reveals how closely your chosen enemy matches the daily enemy across six attributes.

🟩 Green: Exact match.

🟨 Yellow: Partial or close match.

🟥 Red: Incorrect match.

Attributes

  • Family: The enemy's biological family.
  • Games: Which Pikmin games the enemy appears in.
  • Weight: How many Pikmin are needed to carry it.
  • Health: The enemy's HP. (Ranges from 1 to 45000).
  • Attacks: The enemy's possible attacks/effects on pikmin.
  • Movement: Where and how the enemy moves.

For enemies with different stats between games, the latest iteration was used.

For Weight and Health, ↑ means the answer is higher, while ↓ means it's lower.

Yellow Weight means the value is within two weight increments of the answer. Yellow Health means the difference is at most 100 HP or 50% of the target enemy's HP.

Hints

Louie's "Help" unlocks after 3 guesses. The enemy's silhouette unlocks after 6 guesses. Using hints is optional.

A new enemy is available every day!
